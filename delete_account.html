Delete Your Jewish Wisdom Account

Account Deletion Instructions

This page explains how to permanently delete your account and associated data from the Jewish Wisdom app. The instructions below provide the steps required to submit an account deletion request directly from the app.

How to Request Account Deletion

To permanently delete your Jewish Wisdom account, follow these steps:

  1. Open the Jewish Wisdom app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Press the "Settings" section button.
  4. Press the "Profile & Security" button.
  5. Scroll down until the bottom of the page.
  6. Press the "Delete Account" button.
  7. Enter your current password.
  8. Press "Permanently Delete Account".

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

When you permanently delete your account, all data associated with the account is deleted. No account data is retained after the deletion process.

Data Deleted

All data associated with your Jewish Wisdom account is deleted when the account is permanently deleted. This includes the personal information associated with the account, such as:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Religion
  • Information indicating whether you follow the Jewish Diaspora

There is no additional retention period for account data after the account deletion process.

Data Retention

Jewish Wisdom does not retain account data after the account has been permanently deleted. All data associated with the deleted account is removed as part of the account deletion process.
